## Recovery commission

- **15%** on the first ₹50,000 recovered
- **20%** above ₹50,000 recovered
- **Zero advance** — paid only after the money credits your bank account.

We never take a cut of nothing. If we recover nothing, the commission is ₹0.

## Which plan?

- **Starter** — try the tool on one marketplace before committing.
- **Growth** — most serious sellers; weekly audits and AI drafts pay for themselves.
- **Business** — high-volume stores needing an owner and an SLA.
- **Enterprise** — platforms, aggregators and multi-brand operators.
