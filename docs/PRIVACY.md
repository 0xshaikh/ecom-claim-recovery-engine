# Privacy Policy

*Effective 24 October 2026*

We keep this short because there is not much to hide. EcomClaim processes the data you choose to load into the tool so it can find recoverable claims. Nothing else happens.

## Data we collect

- **Seller name** — the name you enter on the dashboard.
- **Email address** — used only for audit exports and support replies.
- **GSTIN** — optional. Only stored if you enter it into the GST-invoice helper.
- **Recovery data you upload** — Orders, Settlement, SKU master and AWB CSVs you load into the local Streamlit app.

## What we do NOT do

- We do **not sell** your data.
- We do **not share** it with third parties, affiliates or data brokers.
- We do **not track** you across sites, on-device or via cross-browser fingerprints.
- We do **not use analytics cookies**, advertising pixels or third-party trackers.

## Retention

Uploaded data is kept for **90 days**, then permanently deleted. Audit exports you generate are yours and live on your own storage.

## Data deletion

To request deletion before the 90-day window expires, email **privacy@ecomclaim.com** from the address you used. We delete within 7 business days and confirm in writing. No reason required.

## Contact

Questions, concerns or regulatory requests: **privacy@ecomclaim.com**
