# Refund Policy

*Effective 24 October 2026*

## Performance-based model

Our core service is **performance-based**: we take a percentage of what we actually recover, only after the money lands in your bank account. Because there is no upfront cost on the recovery fee, **no refund is needed** on that portion — if we recover nothing, you pay nothing.

## Setup fee

Any one-time setup fee is **fully refundable within 7 calendar days**, no questions asked.

## Failed recovery

If the recovery fell materially below the audited estimate we communicated before filing, you get a **full refund** of all fees paid.

## Cooling-off

All plans include a **30-day cooling-off period**. Cancel within 30 days and we refund unused monthly fees.

## How to request

Email **refund@ecomclaim.com** with your account details and reason. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.
