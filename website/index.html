Three Leakage Engines

Every marketplace seller bleeds money in three predictable ways. Each engine targets one leak with an audit trail.

⚖️

Courier Weight Fraud

Couriers over-declare chargeable weight by 5–40% to reach the next billing slab. We recompute dead + volumetric weight per SKU and recover the slab overcharge.

📦

Lost RTO Returns

Reverse shipments stuck in transit beyond 45 days are recoverable under Flipkart SPF and Meesho claims policy. We flag every unreconciled AWB with its invoice value.

🔧

Unpaid Damaged Returns

Damaged RTO parcels that you paid forward shipping on should be settled. Cross-referencing settlement vs. order data surfaces every unpaid damaged return.

How it works

Export your portal data, run locally, get a report. No data ever leaves your machine.

Export Portal Data

Download 90 days of Orders, Settlement, and SKU master CSVs from your Flipkart, Meesho, or Amazon seller hub.

Run Reconciliation

Streamlit dashboard loads your CSVs, runs the three engines, and shows KPIs, charts, and per-AWB claim tables.

Download & File Claims

Export an Excel report with every claimable AWB and generate marketplace-specific claim notes via the AI drafter.
